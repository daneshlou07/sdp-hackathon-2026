# Validated Product Hypothesis: Energy Decision Intelligence Layer

## The Go / No-Go Gate

Based on our comprehensive review of Sime Darby Property's sustainability reports, Inta Bina proposals, and our direct audit of the IESS Cloud platform, we have validated the following business problem and proposed solution:

> Sime Darby Property currently **monitors battery energy storage systems (BESS), solar infrastructure, and building loads through siloed, retrospective dashboards (such as IESS Cloud and Pantas).**
>
> This causes **decision-makers to rely on manual, reactive analysis rather than proactive strategy, making it difficult to optimally orchestrate energy flows (e.g., when to charge/discharge batteries against peak tariffs or excess solar generation).**
>
> Existing system **IESS Cloud** already solves **real-time BESS hardware telemetry monitoring, historical charge/discharge tracking, and device alarm management.**
>
> However, it does not adequately solve **predictive energy forecasting, cross-asset (Solar + BESS + Grid) optimization, or providing actionable financial and carbon impact simulations prior to operational execution.**
>
> Our solution **(The Energy Decision Intelligence Layer)** enables **automated forecasting of energy conditions to generate actionable, financially quantified charge/discharge recommendations.**
>
> This creates **maximized solar utilization, reduced peak demand charges, and accelerated Scope 2 emission reductions** in financial, operational and sustainability value.

---

## Strategic Conclusion

We should **not** build another dashboard. 
We should **not** build an AI chatbot that just reads metrics.

We must build a **decision-support prototype** that demonstrates one complete loop:
1. Forecasts an upcoming energy event (e.g., solar surplus + evening peak).
2. Generates a specific recommendation (e.g., "Charge BESS 12:00-14:00, Discharge 19:00-21:00").
3. Calculates the projected RM savings and CO2 reduction *before* the user approves it.
