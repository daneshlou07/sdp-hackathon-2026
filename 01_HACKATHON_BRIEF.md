# Sime Darby Property University Hackathon 2026 — Hackathon Brief

## Role of this file
This is the high-level competition context. Treat official hackathon information as source facts. Do not invent requirements.

## Official theme
> How might we reimagine the Township of Tomorrow where people, businesses and nature thrive together through innovation, technology and sustainable living?

## Relevant track
### PLANET
Focus areas include:
- Waste management
- Circular economy
- Energy efficiency
- Water conservation
- Green transport
- Biodiversity
- Climate resilience

Our concept is primarily an ENERGY EFFICIENCY / SUSTAINABLE LIVING solution.

## Competition structure
- University students in Malaysia
- Teams of 3–5
- 10–12 week challenge
- Teams are expected to address real business challenges
- Mentoring is provided
- Teams build prototypes
- Finalists pitch to Sime Darby Property leadership
- Strong ideas may be considered for real-world implementation or pilot

## Judging criteria
- Strategic Fit & Theme Relevance — 15%
- Impact & Sustainability Value — 25%
- Innovation & Differentiation — 20%
- Feasibility & Deliverability — 20%
- Commercial & Value Multiplication — 10%
- Team, Evidence & Pitch Quality — 10%

## Winning principle
Do not build a technically impressive product that solves a generic problem.

The project must demonstrate:

1. A real Sime Darby Property problem.
2. Evidence that the problem exists.
3. Awareness of systems Sime Darby Property already uses.
4. A clear gap that existing systems do not adequately solve.
5. A feasible prototype.
6. Measurable financial, energy and sustainability value.
7. A realistic path to pilot deployment.

## Hard rule
Before finalising the product, answer:

> Sime Darby Property currently ________.  
> This causes ________.  
> Existing system ________ already solves ________, but does not adequately solve ________.  
> Our solution ________ enables ________, resulting in ________.

If this cannot be supported by evidence, the problem is not sufficiently validated.
