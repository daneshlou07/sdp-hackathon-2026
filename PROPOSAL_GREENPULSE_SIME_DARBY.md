## Executive Summary

When Sime Darby Property builds a new masterplanned township, the brand promise is sustainable living, community wellness, and low-carbon infrastructure. However, behind the construction hoardings of every new greenfield parcel: from Putra Heights and City of Elmina to Bandar Bukit Raja: lies a costly operational bottleneck: **for the first 9 to 18 months of construction, the entire site is powered by rented diesel generators.**

Permanent Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB) electrical substations take between 9 and 18 months to build, inspect, and energise. Because building works cannot wait, main contractors are forced to rent heavy 300 to 500 kVA diesel generators to operate tower cranes, passenger hoists, site offices, and worker living quarters.

Following Malaysia's diesel subsidy rationalisation, the commercial construction sector is 100% excluded from subsidised fuel. Contractors purchase commercial Euro 5 diesel at the unsubsidised market rate of **RM 5.17 per litre**, burning 12,000 to 18,400 litres every month. Between fuel bills and monthly equipment rental invoices, a single building parcel burns over **RM 100,000 to RM 125,000 every single month in temporary power**.

Compounding this financial bleed, continuous 82-decibel generator drone and diesel exhaust irritate families who have already moved into Phase 1 homes across the street. These disturbances trigger complaints to local town councils (MBSA, MBPJ) and the Department of Environment (DOE), risking statutory Stop-Work Orders that cost developers **RM 50,000 to RM 100,000 per day** in Liquidated Ascertained Damages (LAD).

**GreenPulse is a zero-genset site energy operating system (SiteOS)** that orchestrates containerised Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), temporary rooftop solar PV, heavy site machinery, and community noise compliance into an autonomous, intelligent clean energy microgrid.

### Key Value Metrics at a Glance:
* **RM 125,128 Net Cash Saved Per Month Per Site**: Eliminates 18,400 litres of monthly diesel fuel and cancels primary 500 kVA generator rentals.
* **49.3 Tonnes of Scope 1 CO2e Abated Monthly**: Achieves a 77.6% reduction in construction site carbon emissions, delivering verified **4.12 kg CO2e / m² CFA** for Bursa Malaysia Sustainability Reporting.
* **88% Site Boundary Noise Reduction (-27.9 dBA)**: Drops construction boundary sound from an intolerable 82 dBA down to 54.1 dBA, safely below the 60 dBA government limit under DOE Planning Guidelines Schedule 6.
* **100% Off-Grid Living Dignity for Workers (Act 446)**: Guarantees 24/7 night ventilation fan and lighting uptime in Centralised Labour Quarters (CLQ), eliminating the dangerous 02:00 AM generator shutdown and cutting next-day site accidents.
* **14.2-Month Capital Payback**: Full return on BESS capital during a single 24-month building project, unlocking **RM 9.84 Million in cumulative savings** across a 10-year township fleet rollout.

### A. The 18-Month Utility Substation Gap
In large greenfield township developments, site possession occurs long before permanent electrical distribution grids reach the parcel boundary. Earthworks, bored piling, and substructure concrete placing must proceed immediately to meet handover milestones. Contractors face a mandatory 9 to 18 month period where the parcel operates 100% off-grid.

### B. The Unsubsidised Commercial Diesel Reality (RM 5.17 / Litre)
While targeted transport subsidies under BUDI Madani provide relief at RM 2.10/L for select logistics, the commercial construction industry pays the full gazetted market rate of **RM 5.17 per litre** for commercial Euro 5 diesel. Running a single 300 to 500 kVA genset for 16 to 24 hours daily consumes between 12,000 and 18,400 litres per month, generating a crushing monthly fuel bill of **RM 62,000 to RM 95,000**.

### C. The Inductive Motor Surge Trap (Why Generators Are Oversized)
Why do contractors rent massive 500 kVA generators when average site load is only 60 to 90 kW? 
The answer lies in inductive motor physics:
* When a 50-tonne tower crane begins hoisting a 2.5-tonne bucket of wet concrete, its heavy electric motor draws a momentary startup inrush current **3 to 5 times its nominal rating** (a 45 kW motor spikes to 94 kW for 1.8 seconds).
* On a weak temporary power feed or smaller generator, this momentary surge causes severe voltage sag, tripping the main circuit breaker.
* If a breaker trips mid-lift, wet concrete hardens in the crane skip, creating dangerous structural delays.
* To avoid this operational headache, site supervisors rent oversized 500 kVA generators that idle at only 20% to 30% capacity for 70% of the day, burning excessive fuel while idling.

### D. Phase 1 Resident Friction and Statutory DOE Stop-Work Penalties
Sime Darby Property townships are developed in sequential phases. When Phase 2 begins piling and framing, families are already living in Phase 1 homes across the access road.
* Conventional diesel gensets produce 82 dBA continuous mechanical noise and particulate exhaust.
* Under Malaysian Department of Environment (DOE) Planning Guidelines Schedule 6, maximum permissible sound levels at residential boundaries are **60 dBA during the day** and **45 dBA at night**.
* Residents suffering sleepless nights lodge complaints with town councils (MBSA, MBPJ) or the DOE.
* Regulatory inspections result in official **Stop-Work Orders (SWO)**. Under developer building agreements, site delays trigger Liquidated Ascertained Damages (LAD) of **RM 50,000 to RM 100,000 per day**, destroying project margins.

## 2. Why Existing Hardware Alone Is Not Enough

Sime Darby Property and progressive main contractors (such as Inta Bina Group Berhad) have already begun pioneering clean power hardware:
* Inta Bina has deployed containerised BESS units at **Putra Heights (261 kWh / 100 kW)** and **Melati (522 kWh / 200 kW)**.
* An off-grid solar-battery system has been engineered for the **Rimbayu Centralised Labour Quarters (169 kWp Solar / 261 kWh BESS)**.

However, field audits reveal that **hardware without an intelligent software operating system fails to realise its true value**:
1. **Passive Telemetry Without Intelligence**: Existing platforms (such as IESS Cloud) only act as dumb data viewers. They display an "Online" indicator and raw kilowatt output. They cannot predict weather, cannot buffer motor spikes, and cannot schedule equipment loads.
2. **Tropical Storm Solar Drops**: In Malaysia, afternoon tropical convection creates sudden rainstorms where solar irradiance collapses by 80% in minutes. Without predictive planning, sudden solar loss triggers emergency generator auto-starts.
3. **Fragmented Workflows**: The M&E engineer on site, the licensed Environmental Officer tracking noise, and the HQ finance director tracking diesel invoices operate in complete silos using paper logbooks and WhatsApp messages.
4. **The Single-Project Capex Trap**: Contractors view a RM 350,000 containerised battery as an isolated, high-risk capital expense for a single 2-year job. Lacking a multi-year fleet rollover mechanism across master townships, they fall back on renting diesel generators.

### The Four Core Technical Pillars of GreenPulse:

### 1. Sub-Millisecond Surge Buffer (Solving Crane Inrush)
GreenPulse leverages the millisecond response time of grid-forming battery inverters. When the 50-tonne tower crane starts hoisting and draws a 94 kW inductive kick, GreenPulse directs the BESS to absorb the spike in **less than 4 milliseconds**. The site's primary voltage remains stable, breakers never trip, and the need for a 500 kVA idling generator is completely eliminated.

### 2. Google Weather API Hyperlocal Forecast & Solar Pre-Charging
GreenPulse integrates the **Google Maps Platform Weather API** (`weather.googleapis.com`) combined with local meteorological Doppler radar.
* The system tracks real-time cloud cover, temperature, and convective thunderstorm probabilities specifically for each township parcel (Putra Heights, Setapak, Bandar Rimbayu).
* When Google's weather model detects an afternoon storm window (for example, 14:00 to 16:30 with 85% rain probability), GreenPulse automatically pre-charges the battery to 88% using morning sunshine (08:00 to 11:30).
* When solar drops during the downpour, the stored energy seamlessly carries the tower crane and concrete placing pumps with zero generator auto-starts.

### 3. EchoShield Acoustic Filtering (DOE Schedule 6 Compliance)
GreenPulse connects to Class 1 sound level meters stationed along the site perimeter:
* It continuously monitors perimeter decibels against statutory limits (60 dBA Day, 45 dBA Night).
* Its edge acoustic algorithm separates construction site machinery noise from external public road traffic. If a heavy gravel truck on the external highway causes a spike to 71 dBA, GreenPulse isolates the external signature, verifying that true site noise remained at a compliant 54.1 dBA and preventing false Stop-Work notices.

### 4. Continuous Worker Living Dignity (Act 446 Compliance)
In conventional off-grid Centralised Labour Quarters (CLQ), contractors shut off diesel gensets at 02:00 AM to save fuel. Over 100 to 300 construction workers are left sweltering in 30°C tropical humidity, causing sleep deprivation, heat stress, and severe next-day accident risks. GreenPulse provides dedicated CLQ microgrid telemetry, guaranteeing **100% night fan and lighting uptime** under the Workers' Minimum Standards of Housing and Amenities Act 1990 (Act 446).
