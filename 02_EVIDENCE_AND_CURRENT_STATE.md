# Sime Darby Property Hackathon — Evidence & Current State

## Purpose
This file separates verified/source-derived information from hypotheses. Do not present hypotheses as facts.

---

# 1. Public Sime Darby Property energy context

Public Sime Darby Property reporting indicates:

- FY2025 electricity consumption: 24,921,964 kWh
- FY2025 Scope 2 emissions: 19,290 tCO2e
- FY2025 Scope 1 emissions: 1,029 tCO2e
- FY2025 Scope 3 emissions: 4,284 tCO2e
- Scope 1 + Scope 2 operational carbon is therefore approximately 20,319 tCO2e.
- Sime Darby Property has a 40% Scope 1 + 2 reduction target by 2030.
- Net Zero target: 2050.
- Energy consumption across the asset portfolio is monitored through a centralised digital platform.
- Smart meters are used for granular energy monitoring.
- Sime Darby Property also has an ESG digital dashboard.
- Data owners submit monthly sustainability data, which is reviewed by Sustainability and used for management / Board reporting.
- Sime Darby Property is enhancing internal systems, processes and analytical capabilities to strengthen the link between sustainability metrics and financial reporting.
- Solar capacity reported: 603 kWp across nine sites.
- Renewable generation reported for 2025: 495 MWh.
- Sime Darby Property has experience with solar, BESS, EV charging and energy-efficiency initiatives.
- Elmina Ridge includes integrated solar + BESS homes.
- EV charging infrastructure exists at selected residential/commercial developments.

## Important implication

DO NOT say:

> "Sime Darby Property has no energy monitoring system."

That would contradict the evidence.

Instead, investigate whether there is a gap between:

> monitoring / reporting

and

> predictive analysis / cross-asset optimisation / decision support / investment simulation.

That gap is currently a HYPOTHESIS and must be validated.

# 5. Evidence classification

Use these labels when researching:

### SOURCE FACT
Directly supported by an official/public source or uploaded project material.

### PROJECT-PROVIDED CONTEXT
Information explicitly supplied by the project/team, but not independently verified.

### HYPOTHESIS
A possible problem, gap or opportunity that still requires validation.

### INFERENCE
A conclusion derived from multiple source facts.

### PROTOTYPE ASSUMPTION
Synthetic or assumed data used only to demonstrate the prototype.

Never turn a hypothesis or prototype assumption into a factual claim.

# 6. Current research conclusion

The strongest evidence-backed direction is NOT:

> "Build an energy monitoring dashboard."

The stronger hypothesis is:

> Build an intelligence layer that can turn existing energy data into predictive, prioritised decisions and connect those decisions to financial, energy and carbon outcomes.

This remains a hypothesis until validated with Sime Darby Property stakeholders.
