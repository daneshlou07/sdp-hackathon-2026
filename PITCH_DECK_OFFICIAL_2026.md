## Appendix Slides (Supporting Details)

### Slide 11: Appendix A | Technical Details
* **Inverter & Battery Reading**: The 4G box connects to the inverter over a standard industrial cable (Modbus TCP) and reads power (kW), voltage, and frequency every 1 second. Tracks battery percentage (SOC %), temperature, and health so the battery is never overloaded. When the crane motor starts, the box sends an instant command to deliver power in under 4 milliseconds.
* **Fence Sound Meter Processing**: Digital sound meter on the fence line tracks noise levels every second. Identifies the difference between site machines (cranes, hoists) and noisy highway dump trucks passing by. Calculates official A-weighted decibels (dBA) for certified government compliance records.
* **Safe Offline Backup**: If cellular internet drops, the box keeps 30 days of data in local storage. Local power control keeps running even during internet blackouts. The site never loses power. Automatically uploads all saved data once internet comes back.
